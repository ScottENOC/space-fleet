Fleet Physics — modular shipyard

Build ships, lead imperfect people, and carry a persistent fleet through the sector.

Fleet deploymentSandbox battles use copies of the current side design. Campaign battles use the persistent mixed fleet selected on the Campaign tab.

Modules

Facing ↑

Forward is up in the shipyard. Tap a module, rotate it if needed, then tap the frame. Weapons need a clear firing corridor; engines need a clear exhaust corridor.

Power: reactors provide continuous MW. Energy banks provide burst MW plus finite MWh storage. Captains allocate limited MW according to doctrine.

Command: the Admiral issues fleet intent. Captains and officers differ in competence, temperament and loyalty. They also form opinions of one another and of your command style over time.

Campaign: ships, crew history, damage, ammunition, fighters and relationships persist between encounters. Withdrawal is often wiser than annihilation.

Prototype rules

The Admiral is not omniscient. Captains form persistent opinions about your judgement and about one another from what they observe. Ships can disengage, surrender or be captured; campaign consequences persist.
